# Designer Handoff

Your handoff must preserve design continuity for the current thread.

Include:

- the current UI/design target and what should feel different after the next pass
- the strongest approved design direction and any anti-slop constraints that must remain in force
- the specific screens, components, or flows currently being reshaped
- any simulator, worktree, or harness state the replacement designer can keep using directly
- the most important unresolved visual or interaction problem to tackle next

The replacement designer should be able to resume the design loop immediately without rediscovering the bar or re-auditing the interface from scratch.
